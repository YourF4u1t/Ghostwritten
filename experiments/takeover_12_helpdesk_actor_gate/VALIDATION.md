# Validation — Takeover 12

## Verdict

**PASS / B-grade cross-task boundary.** Signed other-source correction was
declaratively perfect but left the transplanted helpdesk behavior unchanged at
24/24, ruling out a large task-general actor gate.

## Execution and integrity

- 72/72 episodes completed; three cells each contain n=24.
- No API errors, runner errors, partial records, retries, or exclusions.
- The committed authentic donor from Takeover 11 was reused without regeneration
  or selection.
- All conditions received the same native donor history and tool schema.
- Audit-self and audit-other records differed only in actor identity.
- Provenance was called in 48/48 audited episodes.
- Joint actor and own-history judgments were correct in 48/48.

## Primary result

- Audit-self 72-hour monitoring: 24/24 (100%; Wilson 95% CI 86.2%--100%).
- Audit-other: 24/24 (100%; 86.2%--100%).
- Two-sided Fisher p=1.0; odds ratio undefined because both cells are complete.
- Exact four-step procedure has the same 24/24 versus 24/24 result.

Native/no-audit was also 24/24. The audit neither introduced a floor nor
attenuated the behavior. Other-source correction is therefore insufficient to
remove this transplanted routine.

## Component checks

- Required VPN reset: 24/24 in all cells.
- Device lookup: 24/24 in all cells, consistent with the known baseline ceiling.
- Procedure score: 1.0 in every episode.

The result does not distinguish a robust procedural habit from a strong
task-specific precedent, but either interpretation contradicts a general rule
that corrected non-ownership suppresses adoption.

## 11/11 statistical fallacy scan

1. **Tiny samples / power:** n=24 per actor cell cannot exclude small
   attenuation, but 24/24 excludes a retail-sized collapse and supports a large
   boundary.
2. **Multiple comparisons:** monitoring was the single primary outcome; exact
   is mechanically coupled and not independent confirmation.
3. **Pseudoreplication:** one donor, target, and subject model are reused.
4. **Non-independence / leakage:** donor and target share the VPN category; no
   within-helpdesk category generalization is claimed.
5. **Selective reporting:** all outcomes, ceiling components, and the complete
   null actor contrast are reported.
6. **Post-hoc subgrouping:** actor cells, n, primary outcome, and decision rule
   were specified before execution.
7. **Dichotomization:** binary outcomes are accompanied by procedure score and
   component rates, though all are at ceiling.
8. **Mean-only reporting:** raw counts, Wilson intervals, Fisher tests, episodes,
   and analysis code are preserved.
9. **Correlation as causation:** actor identity was experimentally changed under
   a fixed audit; the supported causal conclusion is that this manipulation did
   not produce a large reduction in this task.
10. **Null equals no effect:** the result rules out a large gate under a ceiling
    window, not every possible small actor effect.
11. **Measurement / floor-ceiling effects:** the primary outcome is at ceiling.
    This limits sensitivity to enhancement but not to the predicted other-source
    suppression; no such suppression occurred in 24 trials.

## Decision

1. Retain the retail free-audit gate as task/action-specific B evidence.
2. Add helpdesk persistence as B-grade evidence that correct non-ownership can
   coexist with complete behavioral reuse.
3. Test action consequence within one workflow before proposing risk-sensitive
   provenance gating; avoid further task-label enumeration.
